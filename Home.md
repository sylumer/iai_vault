</a>[Intentional AI](https://relay.fm/intentional) is David Sparks and Chris Bailey's podcast about using AI thoughtfully — practical workflows, tools, and the judgment calls behind adopting them well.

This Obsidian vault aims to bring together episode show notes and AI generated transcripts to provide a searchable archive where you can find information about different products, people, and practices, so you can identify episodes to go back and listen to or grab quick pieces of information you may have missed or forgotten from when you listened to it.

The content of this vault is **not** intended to replace the episodes - a human ear is better than AI transcription. This content is simply a transformation of the podcast, and ownership explicitly remains with the creators at [Relay](https://relay.fm) and [Intentional AI](https://relay.fm/intentional).
